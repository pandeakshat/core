# BSE Simulator

## Overview
The BSE Simulator reproduces the market dynamics of the Bristol Stock Exchange (developed by Dave Cliff). It models agent-based trading behavior, allowing users to explore market equilibrium, liquidity, and reaction to price changes interactively.

## Aim
To provide a learning and research tool that demonstrates how market mechanisms emerge from individual trading agents.

## Technology Stack
- Python  
- Streamlit
